🌿 Guided Breathing Exercises

Follow these step-by-step techniques to reduce anxiety and clear your mind before exams.

1. Box Breathing Method (4-4-4-4)

Best for resetting focus during stressful study sessions.

  1. Inhale slowly through your nose for 4 seconds.
  2. Hold your breath at the top for 4 seconds.
  3. Exhale fully through your mouth for 4 seconds.
  4. Pause and hold empty for 4 seconds before repeating 4 times.

2. 4-7-8 Relaxing Breath

Great before sleep or when feeling overwhelmed.

  1. Place the tip of your tongue against the ridge behind your upper teeth.
  2. Inhale quietly through nose for 4 seconds.
  3. Hold your breath for 7 seconds.
  4. Exhale completely through mouth making a 'whoosh' sound for 8 seconds.
